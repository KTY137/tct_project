The frame

rail · pill shelf · status strip

The audit’s biggest lever: kill the toolbar-first look. Three composed strips replace menu-chrome — brand & connection, navigation, live run state. Detach (⤢) stays a first-class citizen: it’s a product feature, not styling debt.

01 · App rail

Wordmark, connection, five device dots, and right-weighted truth: HV, motion, app state. Cached reads only — the rail never talks to hardware.

02 · Pill shelf

Navigation with confidence: larger pills, calm inactive ink, material active state. Double-click or ⤢ tears any panel into its own window — multi-monitor is a lab reality.

03 · Status strip

Five MetricTiles fed by runState at 1 Hz. Tiles dim when no run is active — staleness is a designed state, not an absence.
