The luminous system-color family (vivid on dark, deepened on light) replaces the 2013 flat set. Semantic colors are never the accent; the accent stays scope-cyan — your instrument's identity — just recalibrated.
Qt honesty, so the pitch is real: colors, radii, type scale, fills, pills, focus rings and press-dim all map 1:1 to QSS (+ a tiny press-scale helper in code). Soft card shadows map to a sparing QGraphicsDropShadowEffect on top-level cards only — never plot containers. The frosted rail is the one aspirational element: true backdrop blur isn't a QSS facility, so the app approximates it with a solid elevated surface in the same tint over the ambient-glow window background (the glow itself is a paintEvent gradient — cheap). Everything else on this page is buildable exactly as shown. If v4 is the direction: P1 ships tokens + QSS, P2 the dot-pulse / ring / numeral-flash motion, P3 the rail. Zero new features, as agreed.
