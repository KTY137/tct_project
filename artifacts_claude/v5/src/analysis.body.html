The calibration rule is non-negotiable: a depletion-voltage line never appears without its fit quality and validity range beside it — an operator must see when the fit is extrapolating.

Decisions

  1. Simultaneous list + preview. Today a QStackedWidget shows either recents or the loaded run. The Finder shape keeps both — switching runs is one click, comparison is a glance.
  2. One status line. "run_00042 · 2 401 pts · map: dut_charge · exported: no" — the four chips were facets of one loaded-run state.
  3. Empty state: "Pick a run" with the recents list already focused — no blank plot slab, no fake extent (map range derives from the run).
  4. CCE fit honesty surfaced as tiles, not buried in a tooltip.
  5. Charge in fC on the colorbar and readouts, consistent with every other panel (storage stays pC).

Law compliance

  • NaN cells stay hatched; no interpolation dressed as data (law 8).
  • Fit results carry quality + validity — calibration rule (law 7).
  • Viridis untouched, readout chips opaque (law 4).
  • Offline analysis never touches hardware — panel has no command classes at all.
