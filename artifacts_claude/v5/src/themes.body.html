How the glass works (and why it's honest)

Qt stylesheets have no backdrop-filter. The cockpit fakes glass exactly the way Apple degrades it under "Reduce Transparency": the chrome and strip surfaces are pre-blended toward the panel color — chrome = blend(tint, panel, amount) — plus a 1-px specular top edge. The slider above drives that blend amount, and the in-app editor drives the identical parameter. Real blur exists in exactly one sanctioned place: a snapshot-once QML effect on the static outer chrome (optional, W3).
