Decisions

  1. Ratified as-is structurally — this panel already implements design system §7. No third metric tile invented; the drift slot only materializes with scan data.
  2. fC at last. The amplitude caption's f"{charge_pC:.3f} pC" prints 0.004 pC for a typical signal — switches to fC (2 dp), labeled raw vs calibrated. Storage stays pC (SCAN_DATA_FORMAT).
  3. Single frame rule. FigureCard's instrument well replaces the GroupBox+plot double bezel.
  4. Stability is a procedure, not a state — the button names its cost ("10 shots"), the tile carries the last result with its timestamp (staleness designed, law 4).

Law compliance

  • Charge displayed at physical scale, labeled raw/calibrated (laws 3/7).
  • Offline plot never draws data-shaped furniture (law 8).
  • Quiet nominal — no status colors while healthy (law 1).
