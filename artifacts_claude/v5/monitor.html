Dim slots, no banner claiming health. "All nominal" may appear only when every configured channel has ≥1 real (non-NaN) reading — Jonathan confirmed the current chip fires with zero readings on file.

Decisions

  1. Per-channel staleness is honest, not noise. Unlike Scan Viewer's four facets of one run, these are independent instruments — each tile carries its own age and dims independently (law 4).
  2. Nominal is silent. The header chip only exists in WARN/ALARM/NO-DATA states; a healthy lab shows four quiet tiles and nothing else.
  3. WARN/ALARM materialized. WARN paints the tile amber + names the threshold + shows the safe-hold consequence; ALARM is the red tile + fail-safe abort banner in the strip. The contract Kaya ratified becomes visible exactly where the excursion lives.
  4. Table becomes a drawer. The hero is the trend; the table is for configuration/inspection, one click away, with per-row status + updated columns preserved.
  5. History empty state: time axis survives (rolling window is real config), y-range never fabricated — "Waiting for first reading."
