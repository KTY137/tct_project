Decisions

  1. Full-bleed well. Today four stacked cards (histogram, beam stats, frame info, view&capture) crush the image to ~40% height. v5: image ~75%, histogram a strip, stats an opaque corner HUD, the rest inspector rows.
  2. Offline is OFFLINE-rung neutral — red implied danger where none exists (law 2 violation, found live).
  3. Streaming is ACTIVE·benign accent — a camera is not motion (amber) and success-green doesn't exist in this cockpit.
  4. Saturation always visible. Paul: clipping ruins measurements silently — chip in the header, amber when above threshold, plus the histogram clip marker.
  5. Stats HUD opaque. Same contrast-floor rule as the scan map — the beam image is data; chips sit on solid plates in the margin.
