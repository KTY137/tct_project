Candidate AMBIENT — The Tiled Cockpit · GlassShell round 02 · Brokkr

The window is glass. The instrument is not. Zero MultiEffect panes, in any window, at any tier. The only translucent surface in the whole application is the window's own unclaimed background — the 8 px gutters. Nothing that carries information ever sits on it, so the FLAT tier is not a degradation: it is this design with the gutters filled in. Flip Tier → FLAT and watch nothing move.

Two switches carry this candidate's whole argument.
1 · Tier. Go SCENE → WINDOW → TOKEN → FLAT. Nothing shifts but the gutters and (at FLAT) the shadow and the specular edge. Every readout, every tone, every contrast ratio is identical. That is the pitch — and it is also the confession that the glass programme is buying very little.
2 · Ladder probe. Switch the card body back to the shipped panel token, in dark, at FLAT. The cards disappear: canvas → panel measures 1.03 : 1 / ΔL* 1.47 in the shipped dark palette. The "three-tone FLAT ladder" round 01 inherited from candidate C does not exist in dark. That is why the new card token is not a nicety — without it, this candidate has nothing at all.
