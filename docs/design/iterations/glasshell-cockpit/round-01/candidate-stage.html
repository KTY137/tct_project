Try the ceremony

State → HV ceremony, or click ⚡ HV… in the capsule. The Hold button stays dead until all three checks are ticked, and it needs a real 2-second press. Three gates: acknowledge, hold, release. Slower than today, on purpose.

The ribbon fills, it does not tint

A cell in an active hazard state becomes an opaque colour block with inverted ink and a glyph. Print it in greyscale and HV-ON still reads as different form, not a different hue. The material never carries this — the fill is painted at every tier.

Where this bets — and where it can lose

It bets that a TCT operator is doing one thing at a time. If they are actually watching bias while aligning, the Peek stubs are too little and this candidate is wrong. That is the question for Kaya, and it is an empirical one.
