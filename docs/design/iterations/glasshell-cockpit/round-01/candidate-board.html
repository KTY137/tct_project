Try “Arrange”

Click ✎ Arrange in the rail. Every card grows a grip — except Safety, whose remove button is disabled and locked. You can move it. You cannot lose it. A composed cockpit that can hide the abort button is not a cockpit.

The board does not take over when a scan runs

A run grows a header in place instead of replacing the screen — because during a scan the operator may still need bias, the stage, or the scope. The layout the operator built is the layout they get, running or not.

Depth ladder: exactly three, forever

container (α .30) → card (α .62) → well (α .55 over card). Blur only on the container and a soft 10 px on cards, so text never sits directly on a blurred backdrop. At Flat these become canvas / panel / well — three opaque tones that keep the same reading order.
