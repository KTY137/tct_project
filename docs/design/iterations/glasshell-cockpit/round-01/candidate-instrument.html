What the tier switch proves

Flip Tier → Flat. Nothing disappears: every number, glyph, stripe, dot and label is painted on the token layer. Glass only removes blur and the specular hairline. The armed rail does not change at all — it is painted with panel, never a material.

What the backdrop switch proves

Backdrop → Worst case is a 100 %-contrast black/white stripe field — the pathological backdrop for translucency. Contrast holds because the panel material's alpha (0.42 dark / 0.72 light) is opaque enough to floor the backdrop. Anything thinner is where legibility dies.

Chrome budget

Today: title bar + menu bar + toolbar + status island + tab shelf = 5 bands, ~13 % of screen height, with the island clipping “Motion” off the right edge. Here: 1 band (88 px), and it is the only thing you must read from across the room.
