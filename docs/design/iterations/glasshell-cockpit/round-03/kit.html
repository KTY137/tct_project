We render the glass; the OS is the garnish. Everything composites against the app-owned ground (layer 0), never the desktop. Toggle Tier → FLAT: nothing is lost, only the tint stops varying with position. Toggle Garnish → DWM on with a worst desktop: watch the contrast meter stay green. That is the whole argument.
